# PETAL---A-Bionic-Butterfly
The bionic butterfly, named petal, is a bio-mech inspired robot project that aims to recreate natural wing movement of a butterfly using simple electronics and lightweight materials such as arduino, servos and batteries along with a carbon fibre rod frame.

Inspiration :
The idea for the Bionic Butterfly came to me after I saw some children in a park playing with butterflies and trying to catch them. It made me curious about whether I could recreate the movement of a butterfly using engineering rather than trying to replicate the entire animal. Later that same day, I came across mechanical design ideas and projects on GitHub, which inspired me to start designing my own version.

Project Overview :
The Bionic Butterfly will use two micro servo motors to create the wing-flapping motion. An Arduino Nano will control the servos, while a small 2S Li-ion/LiPo battery will provide power.
The butterfly will also be controlled using a FlySky radio controller and receiver, allowing the movement to be controlled wirelessly.
The wings will be made from extremely lightweight under-40 GSM thin fabric/paper, keeping the moving parts as light as possible.

Planned Hardware :
- Arduino Nano
- 2 × Micro Servo Motors
- Small 2S Battery
- FlySky Transmitter & Receiver
- Lightweight wing material; <40 GSM
- Custom mechanical linkage and wing mechanism
- 3D-printed/CAD-designed structural components
