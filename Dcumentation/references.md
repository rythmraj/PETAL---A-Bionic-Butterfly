References & Attribution

This project was developed through research into existing bionic butterfly projects, open-source CAD models, electronics implementations, and servo-control techniques.

I have intentionally documented external work used as references so that the original creators are not presented as part of my original work.

---

S-DiY Bionic Butterfly

The S-DiY bionic butterfly project was studied to understand the general concept of a two-servo bionic butterfly and its mechanical/electronic implementation.

The project influenced my understanding of the overall mechanism, but the firmware and implementation in this repository were developed separately.

Original project:

[(https://www.youtube.com/@SDiYchannel01)]

---

Ctorque CAD Model

A Ctorque CAD model was used as a starting/reference model for part of the mechanical design.
I modified the relevant model for my implementation and personalised the design by adding my own identifying/detail features.
Original model:

[https://www.youtube.com/@CTorque]


The repository does not claim the original base geometry as my original work.
