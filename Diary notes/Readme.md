Development Diary

This folder contains photographs/scans of my physical development diary.

I used the diary to record research, ideas, calculations, design decisions, experiments, problems, and changes made during development.

The pages are kept in chronological order where possible.

The diary is included as development documentation rather than as a formal technical specification. The final technical information is maintained in the main project documentation.

Contents

- Research notes
- Initial ideas
- Mechanical design notes
- Servo experiments
- Firmware experiments
- Electronics notes
- Problems encountered
- Design changes
- Testing observations
- Future improvements

The photographs/scans are kept as close to the original notes as possible.
