# Build Procedure

## 1. Print the parts

If you are going with the pointy design, print it, if you are going with fc design, print it as well.

## 2. Prepare the servos

mount the servos while matching the holes present on the body

## 3. Assemble the wing mechanism

use carbon fiber rods (1 to 0.5 mm as supports to assemble the wing) and then connect it to the connector

## 4. Install the Arduino

install the arduino board with the battery on to the body.

## 5. Install the receiver

install the receiver on the electronics as well (MKAE SURE TO REMOVE THE I-BUS OR S-BUS CHANNEL FROM THE RECEIVER, JUST REMOVE THE WIRE)

## 6. Wire the electronics

preferebly solder the wires to reduce weight of extras, connect it as shown on the diagram. 

## 7. Upload firmware

find the firmware on the firmware file in the repo.

## 8. Bind the transmitter

click on the binding button, and when the led on the receiver stops blinking and flashes a hard light, your transmitter is now binded with the receiver

## 9. Test the servos

the servos will move in opposite direction from each other and will change speed, pitch, frequency and angle as you change it on the respective channels on the transmitter.
